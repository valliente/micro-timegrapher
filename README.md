# Micro Timegrapher

> **Acoustic Mechanical Watch Diagnostic Workstation & Escapement DSP Telemetry Engine**

[![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/Platform-Web%20%7C%20Desktop-blue.svg)](https://github.com/valliente/micro-timegrapher)
[![DSP](https://img.shields.io/badge/DSP-Autocorrelation-cyan.svg)](https://github.com/valliente/micro-timegrapher)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-green.svg)](LICENSE)

Micro Timegrapher is an acoustic diagnostic workstation and digital signal processing (DSP) telemetry suite designed for precision analysis of mechanical watch movements. It captures high-frequency acoustic impulses generated by escapement pallet-jewel collisions, extracting operational metrics including daily rate deviation, beat error, and balance wheel oscillation amplitude.

---

## Acoustic Telemetry & Diagnostic Algorithms

```
Acoustic Ingestion (Microphone Interface)
                 │
                 ▼
Bandpass Frequency Filtering (2 kHz - 12 kHz)
                 │
                 ▼
Impulse Peak Detection & Event Windowing
  (T1: Roller jewel strikes pallet fork notch)
  (T2: Pallet jewel impulse phase)
  (T3: Pallet jewel locks onto escape wheel tooth)
                 │
                 ▼
Autocorrelation & Beat Timing Extraction
                 │
   ┌─────────────┼─────────────┐
   ▼             ▼             ▼
Rate (s/d)   Beat Error (ms)  Amplitude (°)
```

### 1. Daily Rate Deviation ($s/\text{day}$)
Measures deviation against nominal balance frequencies ($18,000$, $21,600$, $28,800$, or $36,000\text{ vph}$):
$$\Delta R = \left(\frac{f_{\text{measured}} - f_{\text{nominal}}}{f_{\text{nominal}}}\right) \times 86400\text{ s/day}$$

### 2. Beat Error ($ms$)
Quantifies rotational asymmetry between clockwise and counter-clockwise balance wheel swings by computing the difference between consecutive half-beat intervals:
$$\text{Beat Error} = |T_{\text{tick}} - T_{\text{tock}}|$$

### 3. Amplitude Calculation ($\text{degrees}$)
Determines angular travel of the balance wheel using the elapsed transit time ($t_1 \to t_3$) across the escapement lift angle $\theta$:
$$\text{Amplitude} = \frac{\theta}{\pi \cdot \sin\left(\frac{\pi \cdot t_{\text{lift}}}{T_{\text{half-period}}}\right)}$$

## Positional Analysis & Reporting

- **6-Position Isochronism Profiling**:
  - Dial Up (CH)
  - Dial Down (FH)
  - Crown Up (VB)
  - Crown Down (VG)
  - Crown Left (VL)
  - Crown Right (VH)
- **Multi-Axis Radar Plotting**: Visualizes delta rate variance across gravitational orientations to detect balance wheel poise imbalance and hairspring centering faults.
- **Automated Telemetry Export**: Compiles capture runs into structured JSON datasets and calibration reports.
